# Pi 500 demonstration media

Owner-supplied physical recordings from the 2026-10-27 testing session, selected
for public GitHub documentation at the owner's request. Originals are retained
privately. These are real camera captures, not generated illustrations.

| Asset | Suggested caption | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| [LUODIER still](pi500-luodier-extended-desktop.jpg) | Raspberry Pi 500: video on the LUODIER/EBPSI with a separate HDMI desktop. | Frame at approximately 6 seconds of the supplied clip. The small screen is not TTQ. |
| [LUODIER MP4](pi500-luodier-extended-desktop.mp4) | LUODIER/EBPSI extended desktop and moving video over PanelBridge/Miracast. | Approximately 6.42 seconds; short physical observation, not sustained reliability. |
| [Qshot still](pi500-qshot-wired.jpg) | Qshot V2 non-Pro receiving a wired HCCAST test pattern from Pi 500. | Frame at approximately 3 seconds of the supplied recording. A still alone does not prove motion. |
| [Qshot GIF](pi500-qshot-motion.gif) | Qshot counter advances and wraps while the block moves. | 87 camera frames / 6.09 seconds, looping. Looping is not a new test run. |
| [Qshot MP4](pi500-qshot-wired.mp4) | Original-length camera recording of the Pi 500 wired Qshot test. | Approximately 9.86 seconds; only part of the 30-second, 10 fps-target test. |

The two MP4s preserve the full supplied visual sequences at 540-pixel width;
audio, location/device/time metadata and auxiliary streams were removed. Stills
were extracted from those derivatives. The supplied GIF's image data and timing
are retained; nonessential metadata extensions are excluded. No screen content
was synthesized, replaced or rearranged. Camera frame rate is not a measurement
of screen refresh or received frame rate.

The wide equipment photograph and network-information screenshots remain in the
private evidence set. The selected public frames show the relevant outputs
without publishing the network screens or full-resolution equipment labels.

The owner authorized publication of these recordings. Visible third-party
wallpaper, applications, logos and video retain their respective rights and are
not relicensed by the project's code license. The browser demonstration used
[this owner-selected YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kdemFfbS5H0).
Use captions that identify the device and bounded result; do not describe these
assets as proof of TTQ support, audio, unattended boot or long-term reliability.
